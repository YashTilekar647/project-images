Extracted research figures for the Pre-Harvest Crop Yield & Production Estimation seminar PPT

These are images extracted directly from the provided PDF papers. Filenames include the PDF page number and image dimensions.
Some papers store diagrams as multiple separate image components; therefore, an extracted image may be a panel/component rather than the entire published figure.

Suggested priorities for the PPT:
- YOLO-Granada: detection outputs, growth-stage examples, and model/network figures.
- Fruit tracking & harvest prediction: tracking pipeline, fruit association/tracking visualizations, and prediction result figures.
- Pomegranate size estimation robot: system workflow, segmentation masks, depth/3D projection, and fruit diameter estimation figures.
- Agricultural price forecasting: workflow and price-trend charts (supporting context only).
- Fruit tree lease valuation: pricing model/result charts (supporting context only).
- Agricultural production research: relevant yield/production figures if applicable to the cited paper.

Attribution: retain the original paper title, author(s), DOI/source, and figure number on the slide. Check the source paper's licence before redistributing figures outside the academic presentation.
